# Awesome-University-Management

## Top University Management Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on University ERP, Student Information Systems, Campus Administration, Admissions, Academics, Finance, HR, Student Services & Institutional Operations*

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS/hosted platforms** and **open-source projects** for **University Management, Higher-Education ERP, Student Information Systems (SIS), Campus Management, Student Lifecycle Management, Academic Administration, Admissions, Registration, Finance, HR, Learning Administration and Institutional Operations**.



These systems help universities and colleges manage the complete student and institutional lifecycle, including **prospective-student recruitment, admissions, applications, student records, programs, curriculum, course catalogues, registration, scheduling, attendance, grades, transcripts, advising, financial aid, student accounts, billing, faculty workload, HR, payroll, procurement, facilities, alumni and reporting**.



**Examples** include Ellucian Banner, Anthology Student, Workday Student, Oracle PeopleSoft Campus Solutions, Jenzabar ONE, Unit4 Student Management, CampusCafe, Academia ERP, Classe365 and Populi.



**[Ellucian Banner](https://www.ellucian.com/)** provides a comprehensive higher-education student-information environment covering admissions, advising, degree planning, registration, academic records, student/faculty self-service, course catalogues, scheduling and institutional administration.



**[Anthology Student](https://www.anthology.com/)** is a cloud-based SIS/ERP designed around the student lifecycle while also supporting institutional operations, including finance, financial aid, faculty workload and payroll. Anthology's SIS/ERP business was acquired by Ellucian in 2025/2026, while the product continues to be supported within the Ellucian ecosystem.



**Open-source emphasis**: This repository deliberately gives substantial attention to open-source alternatives and building blocks. A complete open-source one-to-one replacement for Workday Student, Banner or PeopleSoft Campus Solutions is still uncommon. The strongest open ecosystem instead combines **OpenEduCat, Frappe Education, openSIS, RosarioSIS, Fedena, Gibbon, ERPNext, Moodle, OpenProject and other open-source academic, ERP and student-information components**.



**[OpenEduCat](https://github.com/openeducat/openeducat_erp)** is one of the strongest open-source education-ERP candidates, providing modules for admissions, students, academics, exams, attendance, fees, library, facilities, parents, timetables and more. Its current Community Edition repository is LGPL-3.0 licensed.



**[Frappe Education](https://github.com/frappe/education)** is another strong foundation, built on the Frappe/ERPNext ecosystem and covering student/teacher management, admissions, fees, course scheduling, exams, attendance and a student portal.



**[openSIS Classic](https://github.com/OS4ED/openSIS-Classic)** provides an open-source SIS covering student/staff data, courses, scheduling, attendance, grades, transcripts, communication and reporting, with a current Community Edition release.



**[RosarioSIS](https://github.com/francoisjacquet/rosariosis)** and **[Fedena](https://github.com/projectfedena/fedena)** are additional long-running open-source SIS projects. RosarioSIS is GPLv2, while Fedena is Apache 2.0.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites or GitHub repositories.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



> **Note:** The projects below are not all complete Workday Student/Banner/PeopleSoft equivalents. They range from full open-source education ERPs and SIS platforms to LMS, admissions, ERP, identity and integration components that can collectively form a university-management ecosystem.



### Full Education ERP / University Management



* **[OpenEduCat Community Edition](https://github.com/openeducat/openeducat_erp)**

  **Open-source education ERP — strongest overall candidate.**

  Comprehensive education ERP based on Odoo, with modules for admissions, student information, courses, batches, classrooms, examinations, attendance, fees, library, facilities, parents, timetables, communication and reporting. The current Community Edition repository is LGPL-3.0 licensed.



* **[Frappe Education](https://github.com/frappe/education)**

  **Open-source education management system.**

  Education-management application built on Frappe/ERPNext. It includes student and teacher management, admissions, fees, course scheduling, examination planning, attendance and a student portal, with ERPNext providing the underlying accounting, inventory and enterprise infrastructure.



* **[openSIS Classic](https://github.com/OS4ED/openSIS-Classic)**

  **Open-source Student Information System.**

  Web-based SIS covering student and staff data, course management, scheduling, attendance, grades, teacher gradebook, progress reports, transcripts, communication and bulk imports. The repository currently lists Community Edition 9.3, released in June 2026.



* **[RosarioSIS](https://github.com/francoisjacquet/rosariosis)**

  **Open-source Student Information System.**

  Full-featured web-based SIS covering students, teachers, administrators, attendance, grades, billing, reports, plugins and communications. Released under GPL-2.0.



* **[Fedena](https://github.com/projectfedena/fedena)**

  **Open-source school/campus management system.**

  Ruby-on-Rails-based education-management system covering student and campus records and a broad range of school-management functions. The repository is Apache License 2.0.



* **[Gibbon](https://github.com/GibbonEdu/core)**

  **Open-source school management platform.**

  Modular web-based education-management platform covering student information, attendance, timetables, activities, assessments, finance-related workflows and school administration. It is particularly useful as a foundation for institutions that want to customize an education-management system.



### Academic / Learning Management



* **[Moodle](https://github.com/moodle/moodle)**

  **Open-source Learning Management System.**

  One of the world's most widely used open-source LMS platforms. It provides courses, assignments, grading, quizzes, learning activities, authentication and integrations. It is not an SIS, but is a critical component of an open university technology stack.



* **[Open edX](https://github.com/openedx/edx-platform)**

  **Open-source learning platform.**

  Scalable open-source learning platform suitable for universities offering online courses, programs and digital learning experiences.



* **[Canvas LMS](https://github.com/instructure/canvas-lms)**

  **Open-source LMS.**

  Open-source learning-management platform supporting courses, assignments, grading, communication, integrations and academic learning workflows.



* **[Chamilo LMS](https://github.com/chamilo/chamilo-lms)**

  **Open-source LMS.**

  Learning-management system providing courses, assessments, learning paths, communication and learner-management capabilities.



* **[ILIAS](https://github.com/ILIAS-eLearning/ILIAS)**

  **Open-source LMS.**

  Mature learning-management platform supporting courses, assessments, learning objects, collaboration and user management.



### Admissions, CRM & Student Lifecycle



* **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**

  **Open-source ERP foundation.**

  Provides accounting, HR, payroll, CRM, purchasing, assets, projects, inventory and other enterprise functions that can complement a university SIS.



* **[SuiteCRM](https://github.com/SuiteCRM/SuiteCRM)**

  **Open-source CRM foundation.**

  Useful for prospective-student recruitment, admissions CRM, outreach, applicant communications and relationship management.



* **[EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm)**

  **Open-source CRM.**

  Can provide admissions/recruitment CRM capabilities around an open SIS.



* **[Odoo Community](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**

  **Open-source ERP/application framework.**

  Provides accounting, HR, CRM, inventory, purchasing and workflow functionality. OpenEduCat extends this ecosystem with education-specific functionality.



### Student Services & Campus Operations



* **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**

  Useful for finance, HR, payroll, procurement, assets and institutional administration surrounding a student-management system.



* **[Frappe Framework](https://github.com/frappe/frappe)**

  Open-source full-stack framework suitable for developing custom university modules, APIs, workflows, portals and integrations.



* **[OpenProject](https://github.com/opf/openproject)**

  Open-source project-management platform useful for university projects, accreditation, curriculum initiatives, research administration and institutional planning.



* **[Snipe-IT](https://github.com/snipe/snipe-it)**

  Open-source asset-management system that can manage university IT equipment, laboratory equipment and other institutional assets.



* **[GLPI](https://github.com/glpi-project/glpi)**

  Open-source IT asset and service-management platform useful for campus IT, helpdesk, equipment and service workflows.



### Timetabling & Academic Scheduling



* **[FET](https://github.com/rodneyb/fet)**

  **Open-source timetable generator.**

  Automated timetable scheduling software capable of handling complex educational scheduling constraints.



* **[UniTime](https://github.com/UniTime/unitime)**

  **Open-source university scheduling system.**

  University-focused system for course timetabling, examination scheduling and student scheduling. It is one of the most relevant open-source components for institutions looking to reproduce the scheduling capabilities of enterprise SIS platforms.



* **[OptaPlanner](https://github.com/apache/incubator-kie-optaplanner)**

  **Constraint-optimization engine.**

  Useful for university course scheduling, examination timetabling, room allocation, staff scheduling and other complex optimization problems.



### Identity, Integration & APIs



* **[Keycloak](https://github.com/keycloak/keycloak)**

  **Open-source identity and access management.**

  Supports SSO, OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect, SAML and role-based access control. Useful for university-wide authentication spanning SIS, LMS, library, portals and research systems.



* **[Apache Kafka](https://github.com/apache/kafka)**

  **Event-streaming platform.**

  Useful for connecting admissions, SIS, LMS, finance, HR, library, identity and analytics systems through event-driven architectures.



* **[Apache Camel](https://github.com/apache/camel)**

  **Integration framework.**

  Useful for connecting university applications, databases, APIs, ERP, SIS, LMS and external services.



* **[Mirth Connect / NextGen Connect](https://github.com/nextgenhealthcare/connect)**

  **Integration engine.**

  Although primarily healthcare-oriented, its HL7/integration architecture can provide useful patterns for institutions handling complex legacy-system integration.

## Recommended Open-Source University Management Stack



For organizations wanting to build an **open-source alternative to Ellucian Banner, Anthology Student, Workday Student, Oracle PeopleSoft Campus Solutions, Jenzabar ONE, Unit4 Student Management, CampusCafe, Academia ERP, Classe365 or Populi**, a practical architecture could combine:



**Student Information System / Education ERP**



`OpenEduCat / Frappe Education / openSIS`



↓



**Student Lifecycle**



`Prospect → Applicant → Admission → Enrollment → Registration → Progression → Graduation → Alumni`



↓



**Academic Administration**



`Programs + Curriculum + Courses + Sections + Faculty + Classrooms`



↓



**Scheduling**



`UniTime / FET / OptaPlanner`



↓



**Student Records**



`Academic History + Grades + Transcripts + Attendance + Advising + Degree Audit`



↓



**Learning Management**



`Moodle / Open edX / Canvas / ILIAS`



↓



**Finance / ERP**



`ERPNext / Odoo Community`



↓



**Admissions / CRM**



`SuiteCRM / EspoCRM`



↓



**Identity & Campus SSO**



`Keycloak + LDAP/AD + OAuth2/OIDC + SAML`



↓



**Integration Layer**



`Apache Kafka + Apache Camel + REST APIs + Webhooks`



↓



**Data Warehouse / Analytics**



`PostgreSQL + Apache Superset / Metabase + Grafana`



A particularly strong open-source combination is:



**OpenEduCat + Frappe Education/ERPNext + Moodle + UniTime + Keycloak + SuiteCRM + Apache Kafka + PostgreSQL + Metabase**



For a smaller institution, a simpler deployment could be:



**Frappe Education + ERPNext + Moodle + Keycloak**



For a university requiring more sophisticated scheduling:



**OpenEduCat + UniTime + Moodle + ERPNext + Keycloak**

## How to Contribute



1. Fork the repo.

2. Add/edit entries in `README.md` (follow existing format).

3. Include: name, GitHub/official link, 1–2 sentence description, and whether it is **SIS, ERP, LMS, CRM, scheduling, finance, HR, student-services or integration infrastructure**.

4. Clearly distinguish between a **complete university-management platform** and a **component that can be used to build one**.

5. Prefer actively maintained projects with clear licenses and documentation.

6. Mention the license where it materially affects self-hosting or commercial use.

7. Identify whether the project is primarily designed for **K-12, higher education, universities or general education**.

8. Submit PR with a short explanation.



Star the repo if you find it useful!



## Disclaimer



* This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

* University ERP/SIS platforms are substantially more complex than ordinary school-management systems.

* **There is currently no broadly established open-source one-to-one replacement for the full breadth of Ellucian Banner, Workday Student, PeopleSoft Campus Solutions or Jenzabar ONE.**

* Open-source projects listed here should therefore be viewed as **alternatives, complementary systems or building blocks** rather than direct replacements.

* Some projects such as RosarioSIS, Fedena and openSIS originated with broader school-management use cases, so universities may require substantial customization.

* OpenEduCat and Frappe Education are particularly relevant to higher-education-oriented open-source stacks, but their feature coverage and maturity differ from enterprise commercial SIS products.

* University systems contain highly sensitive student, financial, academic and personnel information. Self-hosted deployments require appropriate authentication, authorization, encryption, backups, disaster recovery, logging and privacy controls.

* Institutions should evaluate applicable privacy and education regulations, accreditation requirements, records-retention rules and local legal obligations before deployment.

* Always verify project activity, license terms, security posture, integrations, data-export capabilities and production readiness before selecting an open-source component.
